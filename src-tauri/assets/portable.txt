快贴便携版

有这个文件，快贴就以便携模式运行：历史记录、设置和日志都保存在同一文件夹的 data 目录里，不写入系统目录。
请不要删除或改名这个文件，否则快贴会改用系统目录（%LOCALAPPDATA%），看起来就像记录丢了。
升级：在“偏好设置 → 关于 → 检查更新”里直接更新；或下载新的便携包，只替换 KwikPaste.exe。

KwikPaste Portable

This file makes KwikPaste run in portable mode: history, settings and logs stay in the data folder next to it, and nothing is written to system folders.
Do not delete or rename this file, or KwikPaste will switch to the system folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%) and your history will seem to be gone.
To upgrade, use Preferences > About > Check for updates, or download a new portable package and replace only KwikPaste.exe.
